How it works

Steer
Touch the screen and drag. Your square heads the way you drag, in any direction, and bounces off walls.
Slow time
While your finger is down the game runs at quarter speed. Let go and it snaps back.
Race
Be the first square into the red zone. Spikes, saws and TNT stun you for a second.
Battle
Ram rivals nose-first to take a life. If one hits your side, you lose one. Shove a rival and hazards hurt it too.
Gunfight
Reach the gun first. It fires three shots at the nearest square, then drops and can be grabbed again a second later. Only bullets hurt in these rounds.
Heat round
Touch the flame first. You keep its ring for the whole round, and it melts any square it reaches, whatever lives that square has.
Boss race
A big boss guards the red zone, and a square it gets is out of the race. Volt shocks every square inside its ring. Frost throws ice shards along its red aim lines. Sarge chases you until he runs out of breath. The strip at the bottom of the boss room is safe.
Boss duel
Just you and a boss. You start with the heat ring and have to get it onto him a few times. Volt sends sparks along a red strip. Frost throws ice along red lines, and a hit freezes you for a moment. Every hit costs a life.
Stars
Some levels hide shiny stars. Touch one to collect it. Can you find them all?
Bubbles
Grab a bubble and it floats around you. The next zap, ice shard or spike just pops the bubble instead of you. It grows back after a few seconds.
Portals
Drive into a swirl and you pop out of the other swirl with the same colour. Rivals can use them too.
Rivals
The other three squares travel on diagonals and turn every time they hit a wall or a square.
